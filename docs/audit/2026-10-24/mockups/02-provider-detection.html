Provider field and network detection

The setup card stacks a district select (“Filter providers by district”), a 2,928-row combobox, a detection card (“Detected NET Express Online’s network. On a local ISP? Choose it above.” + button) and a second hint once chosen. The card arrives ~2 s after paint and pushes the connection tiles down (CLS 0.03). Filtering by the wrong district hides the real ISP, so people type it and create duplicates (e.g. “starlink” in Sylhet → “Add “starlink””).
