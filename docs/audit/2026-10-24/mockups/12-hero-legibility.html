Keeping the hero headline legible over the live map

The Doto headline and lede sit straight on the street map, so Bengali and English place labels (Khagan, Banagram, Savar…) run through “HOW FAST IS YOUR”. At 1024 px the text overlaps dense labels. The map is the flair; it just needs to step back behind the copy.

Use a no-labels style for the hero map only, so roads and rivers stay and the text never fights place names. Clean, but you lose the “this is Dhaka” cue the labels give.

Recommendation: B: a dither mask. It fixes the legibility with the site’s own visual language instead of a plain fade.

Frames are static mockups in the site’s tokens (Space Grotesk, Doto, Geist Mono, ink #191a23, lime #b9ff66, hard 5px shadows, dither dots). Where live data was thin, names and numbers are illustrative.
