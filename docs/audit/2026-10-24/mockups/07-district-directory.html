District pages: local providers first

/district/bandarban says “193 providers · Page 1 of 4”, but 192 are nationwide licences listed first in id order, and Bandarban’s only local ISP isn’t on page 1. Every district looks the same, so the page doesn’t help anyone choose. The table also shows “Nationwide · Nationwide licence” in two columns.
