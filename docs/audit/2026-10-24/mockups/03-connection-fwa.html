Connection choice and the gpfi home-router declaration

Picking gpfi reveals an unchecked checkbox, “5G/4G home router (e.g. gpfi) / Check this only for the home router, not phone data or a hotspot.” gpfi is a fixed-wireless-only product, so most gpfi testers are on the router. Forgetting the tick means the result is flagged as mobile data and silently doesn’t count.
